## Teach first (about 10 minutes)
Open these scenario fixtures, in order:
1. **Who can pollinate what.** Click the Bee, then the Wind. Open the ⓘ on each tree.
2. **From Copper to Signet.**
3. **Feast for a second wave.**
4. **Mould returns the dead to the soil.**

Before moving on, answer aloud: "Why can the Bee pollinate the Pear but not the Hazel?"

## Ask at two moments in each match (privately)
- What is the opponent trying to make?
- What ordinary move could stop it?
- What would stopping it cost you?
