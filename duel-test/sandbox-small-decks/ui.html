Word Alchemy small-deck sandbox

A separate test table for small decks, themes and the graveyard. It shares nothing with the live game and keeps no save.

Market (bought with spark)
How this sandbox plays
  1. Bring the rival from 30 life to 0. Breath grows by 1 each turn, up to 7. You draw up to two words a turn, never above your hand size.
  2. A thing stays on your row. It can strike once a turn, but not on the turn it arrives unless it is swift. A thing that strikes is not on guard during the rival’s next turn; a thing that waits is.
  3. When a thing strikes the rival, the rival’s sturdiest thing on guard stands in the way and takes the blow. Only flyers (and things with reach) stop flyers. A guardian always steps in first and takes 1 less.
  4. An action is given to one of your things, and it remembers up to two. A quality stays on a thing. A weather lasts three of its caller’s turns. A spell is spoken and goes to your discard, or to the graveyard if it says single use.
  5. Your deck is small and it comes round again: at the start of your turn, if it has run out, your discard is shuffled back in. Nothing you say comes back within the same turn. Destroyed things go to the graveyard and do not come back by themselves. With the graveyard switched off they go to your discard instead.
  6. Once a turn you may fuse two things that have a recipe, from your hand or your row, for the breath shown. Ladders climb on their own: seed to sapling to oak, lead to tin to silver to gold.
  7. Once a turn you may discard a word to draw another.
  8. The first spell of The Word you speak each turn also hits the rival for 2, plus 1 for each speaker on your side (up to 3).
  9. From round 11 the Fading takes life from both sides, so no duel stalls.
